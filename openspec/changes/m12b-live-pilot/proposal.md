# Controlled authorized OpenCode live pilot

## Why
The offline-verified M1.2a boundary needs version-specific transport evidence and
an explicitly scoped real-provider execution connected to independent grading.
An observed process completion is neither task success nor complete billing.

## What Changes
- Freeze a retrying single-agent experiment and compare explicit operator
  authorization at the provider-capable execution boundary.
- Audit installed OpenCode 2.3.0 and prove supported interception semantics with
  loopback fixtures; reject unsupported required hard guarantees.
- Isolate solver configuration and preserve requested versus observed identities,
  partial accounting, lifecycle, immutable submissions, and independent regrades.
- Exercise the authorized exact OmniRoute `cx/gpt-6-luna-low` route only after
  offline gates, without invented token/request/run/financial quotas.

## Impact
Reuse Ruby, SQLite, the CLI adapter, artifact storage and audited-v2 grader.
No general gateway, distributed quota service, benchmark modification, fallback,
hidden solver material, push, deployment or merge.
