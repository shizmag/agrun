## Why

M0.1 verifies execution, not external research measurements. Add reusable pinned
benchmark infrastructure with honest unavailable outcomes and independent grading.

## What Changes

- Benchmark registry, public tasks, fixture solvers and immutable experiment plans.
- Pinned NL2Repo dataset lifecycle and native protocol audit.
- Fail-closed container grading boundary and evidence integrity checks.
- Persisted matrix execution, regrading, deterministic JSON/CSV/Markdown reports.
- Offline acceptance and adversarial tests, explicit official validation target.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `scientific-benchmarks`: reproducible independent benchmark orchestration.

### Modified Capabilities

None; preserve M0.1 APIs and guarantees.

## Impact

Ruby benchmark modules, CLI, mise targets and documentation. No providers, image
downloads in tests, distributed service, paid calls, push or main merge.
