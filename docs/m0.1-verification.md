## Scientific readiness and remaining boundaries

Generation, evaluator execution and quality verdict are distinct. Submitted
evidence is checked against current repository, specification, procedure,
environment and evaluator identity. Historical reports are not automatically
current validation. Standalone identical content cannot establish independent
solver lineage without an expected generation.

Directory/process-group isolation is not an OS sandbox. No hostile concurrent
filesystem/DB rewrite, TOCTOU or authenticated build identity is guaranteed.
Source identity covers one file, not transitive dependencies; evaluator version
discipline remains necessary. Custom trusted Ruby cancellation is cooperative.
Process-group ID reuse/signal-error risk is mitigated, not eliminated; ordinary
successful completion does not terminate background descendants that closed their
captured streams, and escaped process groups are outside supervision guarantees.
The independent reviewer found no demonstrated blocker in the tested scope.

No recovery/resume, HTTP transport, official grader, memory isolation, live-provider
validation or provider-side budget enforcement was added. Offline fixtures made
no paid/provider calls, but there is no network-enforced offline boundary.
Completed offline runs can be reconstructed from retained metadata; partial
records do not promise recovery. Missing usage stays unavailable, not observed
zero. The deterministic demo is plumbing conformance, not agent-capability data.
