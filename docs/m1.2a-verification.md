## Gate 2 — contract review

The independent reviewer conditionally approved the additive interfaces, requiring:

- Native `(sessionID, messageID)` identifies an observed root model step, **not**
  a proven provider request. Deduplication is attempt-scoped.
- Native `part.cost` may be computed by OpenCode; preserve it as opaque CLI cost,
  not a provider-reported charge. Currency and provenance cannot be invented.
- Root stream coherence does not prove child/background billing coverage.
  Invocation accounting remains partial/unknown even with exit zero.
- Immutable raw artifact evidence is authoritative; compact attempt summaries are
  indexes, not reconstruction oracles. Disagreement must remain visible.
- Host-injected shared coordinator scopes are process-local, not durable quotas.
- Legacy `live` booleans cannot authorize an executable. Offline authorization
  must bind an audited absolute fixture path and SHA256, checked before spawning.

These corrections are binding implementation/test contracts. The parser and
accounting helpers are additive; Result and the independent grader remain intact.
