The adapter forwards the path (`lib/agent_runtime/adapters/sandbox_open_code_cli.rb:20-30`), while `Submission#capture` (`lib/agent_runtime/benchmarks/submission.rb:9-22`) stores and binds the actual content it receives, not the runner's export digest. ## Independent review prerequisites for the eight release gates

Do not sign based on gate booleans, self-assertions, or the `release` API passing. Inspect the evidence artifacts themselves and their content hashes against the exact candidate and final commit, with independent reviewer distinct from producer. For `a_pass` and `b_pass`, require actual completed plugin/LSP positive and disabled controls plus their respective live mutation results and no unexpected errors/skips. For `post_commit_fresh_parity`, require newly executed installed-image parity on the final clean commit and digest, not pre-commit evidence. For `semantic_architectures_1_3_3`, inspect equivalent task/model/procedure, role topology and per-architecture traces, not merely configured counts. For `immutable_submission_grade_regrade`, trace the captured submission digest through independent grade and regrade results and confirm no source changes. For `writer_quiescent_export`, inspect successful quiescence, cleanup and actual exported bytes/digest with adversarial tests. For `effective_environment_verified`, compare image and binary identity and the actual effective launch configuration, resource/deadline limits, benchmark/task and audited grader files with the bound documents and execution observations, including inherited configuration controls. For `no_execution_changes`, verify clean final commit plus hashes of every execution-affecting binding and no edits since evidence runs. Check artifact provenance and scope; mock parity cannot establish live capability. Missing/incomplete artifacts or incomplete adversarial mutation coverage means withhold attestation.
